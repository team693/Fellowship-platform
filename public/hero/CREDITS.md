## Provenance, honestly

None of these were shot in Pakistan except the bazaar and they are stock, not
Heal's own fieldwork. Unsplash has very little Pakistani material of this kind.
If that matters for a given panel, replacing it with a commissioned photograph
is a drop-in swap.

## Why these and not others

The brief was material and place-led rather than hardship-led. Unsplash has a
large, well-shot set from a rural health camp — identifiable patients, mostly
elderly women, mid-treatment. Those were deliberately not used. A stock licence
grants no model release, and a queue of patients is not marketing material for a
programme whose own content rules say communities have agency and should be
framed that way. The people who do appear are working, building or being served
in public settings, at angles where nobody reads as a spokesperson endorsing the
programme.
