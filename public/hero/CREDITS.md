## Why these and not others

The brief was material and place-led rather than hardship-led. Unsplash has a
large, well-shot set from a rural health camp — identifiable patients, mostly
elderly women, mid-treatment. Those were deliberately not used. A stock licence
grants no model release, and a queue of patients is not marketing material for a
programme whose own content rules say communities have agency and should be
framed that way.
